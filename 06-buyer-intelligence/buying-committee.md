# Buying Committee: Potential Restate Evaluation

Titles and ownership vary by company. This map is a hypothesis to guide research, not a claim about Ramp's org chart.

## 1. Technical user

**Possible titles:** Senior Backend Engineer, Software Engineer, AI Engineer.

**Primary concerns:**
- Developer experience.
- Reliability of workflows.
- Ease of implementing retries and recovery.
- Debugging and testing.
- Fit with the current stack.

**Role in the process:** Uses or prototypes the technology; may become an internal champion.

## 2. Technical champion / evaluator

**Possible titles:** Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer, Senior Platform Engineer, AI Infrastructure Lead.

**Primary concerns:**
- Architecture fit.
- State and concurrency semantics.
- Failure recovery.
- Integration and migration cost.
- Operational model and performance.

**Role in the process:** Evaluates whether the product solves a real technical problem and can influence the engineering decision.

## 3. Operational decision-maker

**Possible titles:** Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering, Head of Platform.

**Primary concerns:**
- Production ownership.
- Reliability and incident response.
- Team capacity.
- Maintainability.
- Standards and deployment risk.

**Role in the process:** Determines whether the change is operationally worthwhile and whether the team can support it.

## 4. Economic buyer

**Possible titles:** VP Engineering, CTO, or relevant engineering executive.

**Primary concerns:**
- Engineering velocity.
- Product delivery.
- Reliability of important customer workflows.
- Infrastructure cost and strategic risk.
- Opportunity cost of maintaining internal systems.

**Role in the process:** Sponsors or approves investment, depending on company size and procurement model.

## Stakeholder strategy

- Start with the person closest to the workflow.
- Learn who owns the runtime and architecture.
- Engage technical evaluators with precise questions.
- Involve leadership when the problem and impact are clear.
- Do not assume the most senior title is the correct first contact.
