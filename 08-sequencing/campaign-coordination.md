# Campaign Coordination and Operating Rules

## Account-level objective

Learn whether a relevant Ramp engineering team owns long-running workflow execution and whether it has a problem worth exploring. A meeting is useful only if there is a plausible workload and an appropriate owner.

## Contact roles

| Contact | Primary objective | What to learn |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Backend / AI Engineer | Understand day-to-day workflow implementation | Current state, retry, and recovery approach |
| Staff / Principal Engineer | Understand architecture and trade-offs | Runtime ownership, failure semantics, evaluation criteria |
| Engineering Manager / Director | Understand operational priority | Maintenance burden, reliability priorities, team impact |
| VP Engineering / CTO | Understand strategic relevance | Whether workflow infrastructure is a platform investment |

## Coordination principles

1. Research each contact's actual role before outreach.
2. Use a different message angle for each persona.
3. Avoid contacting too many people at once with identical copy.
4. Update account notes when new information is learned.
5. Do not assume one contact speaks for the entire engineering organization.
6. Ask permission before sharing a contact's non-public comments with another stakeholder.
7. If one person confirms that another team owns the problem, redirect thoughtfully.

## CRM fields to track

- Account name
- Contact name and title
- Persona / buying role
- Source of contact information
- Public evidence / personalization
- Hypothesis being tested
- Last touch date
- Channel
- Response status
- Objection or current solution
- Next action
- Opt-out / do-not-contact status

## Suggested qualification stages

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Researched | Public evidence collected; no contact yet |
| Contacted | Relevant first touch sent |
| Engaged | Recipient has replied or interacted meaningfully |
| Discovery | Current workflow and ownership are being explored |
| Qualified | Relevant workflow, pain, owner, and plausible timing identified |
| Nurture | Potential fit but no current priority |
| Disqualified | No meaningful workload fit or no relevant need |

## Metrics

Track:
- Accounts researched.
- Contacts verified.
- Personalized first touches sent.
- Positive reply rate.
- Discovery conversations booked.
- Qualified conversations.
- Referral rate to the correct owner.
- Reasons for disqualification.

Do not optimize solely for activity volume. A small number of well-researched, relevant conversations can be more valuable than high-volume generic outreach.

## Compliance and professionalism

Use legitimate business contact data and follow applicable privacy, electronic marketing, and telemarketing rules. Respect opt-outs and channel preferences. Do not use personal contact information or automated calling without an appropriate legal basis.
