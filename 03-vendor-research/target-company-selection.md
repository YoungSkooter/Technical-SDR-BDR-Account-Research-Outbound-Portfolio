# Target Company Selection

## Objective

Select accounts where Restate's durable-execution capabilities may map to a real engineering workload, rather than building a list from company size or technology keywords alone.

## Account evaluation framework

| Dimension | What to look for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow complexity | Multi-step, long-running, asynchronous processes | Core durable-execution relevance |
| External dependencies | Multiple APIs, tools, services, integrations | More failure and coordination boundaries |
| AI / automation | Agents, tool calls, human approval | Often creates long-running execution patterns |
| Engineering maturity | Backend/platform/distributed-systems team | Ability to evaluate infrastructure |
| Business criticality | Financial, customer, or operational workflows | Reliability may have meaningful impact |
| Trigger | Product launch, scale, hiring, architecture evolution | Reason to engage now |
| Buyer access | Relevant engineering leaders identifiable | Practical path to discovery |

## Account shortlist exercise

The initial shortlist considered:
1. **Ramp** — financial operations and accounting automation; strong potential workflow relevance.
2. **Harvey** — legal AI workflows; potential relevance if production AI processes require durable multi-step execution.
3. **Lyzr** — AI agent infrastructure; conditional fit because it may already provide overlapping orchestration capabilities.

## Prioritization

Ramp was selected for deeper research because its public product context provided a concrete workflow to investigate. Harvey remained a plausible secondary account. Lyzr required more careful competitive qualification because its own product category may overlap with the problem Restate addresses.

## Selection discipline

A company can be a strong ICP match without having active buying intent. Keep these concepts separate:
- **Fit:** The product category could be relevant.
- **Evidence:** Public facts suggest a related workload.
- **Pain hypothesis:** A plausible problem may exist.
- **Intent:** The account is actively evaluating or trying to solve it.

Only claim intent when there is direct evidence.
